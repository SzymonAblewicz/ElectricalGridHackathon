\begin{frame}{1. A new shift factor, $S_{e,n}$}

The change in flow on branch $e$ per MW curtailed at wind node $n$.
Positive means relief, negative means it loads the branch further.

\bigskip
\begin{equation*}
\text{PTDF} = BK\T\Lp, \qquad
S_{e,:} = \text{PTDF}_{e,:}\cdot\operatorname{sign}(f_e)
\end{equation*}

\bigskip
A $\gne\times\gnb$ matrix, built once from topology and reactance ---
$\gncong$ branches reach their rating over the week, $\gncongtx$ of them
transformers.

\end{frame}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{2. Why it's better}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{EirGrid:} perturb one farm by 10\,MW, re-run the load
        flow, read the change. One number per run.
  \bigskip
  \item \textbf{This method:} an analytic derivative --- all
        $\gne\times\gnwind$ branch--node pairs at once, exact to the DC
        model.
  \bigskip
  \item \textbf{Verified} against an independent solver (PyPSA):
        residual $= \gresid$\,MW.
\end{itemize}

\end{frame}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{3. The issue with SF constraint groups, and the fix}

EirGrid's group: $\{\,n : S_{e,n} \ge \tau\,\}$, $\tau = \pTau$ ---
one row, read in isolation. Blind to every other branch.

\medskip
Net sensitivity alone made it \textbf{worse}: \pOvlBrokeEvents\ events
broke a line, against \pEirBrokeEvents\ for the plain method.

\medskip
\textbf{The veto:} cap each node's cut at the point where any other
live branch would be pushed over its rating.

\bigskip
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lrr}
\toprule
& \textbf{cleared} & \textbf{broke a line}\\
\midrule

\end{center}
\note{\pN\ (outage, hour) events.}

\end{frame}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{4. Fixed for every N--1 contingency, computed once}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Group membership depends only on topology and reactance ---
        not hour, not weather.
  \bigskip
  \item Precomputed for all \pTripsOutages\ outages $\rightarrow$
        \pTripsCircuits\ circuits ever at risk, \pTrips\ lookup rows.
  \bigskip
  \item \pVetoedFromEirPct\% of EirGrid's own group memberships fail
        the veto and are excluded before dispatch.
  \bigskip
  \item Online, only the live flow and the final MW instruction are
        computed.
\end{itemize}

\end{frame}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{5. Farms that can run full blast}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Top 5 nodes account for \pTopFivePct\% of all MW ever
        curtailed; top 20 for \pTopTwentyPct\%.
  \bigskip
  \item \pRareN\ of \gnwind\ nodes --- \pRareFleetPct\% of fleet
        capacity --- are cut in under 1\% of events.
  \bigskip
  \item Across the entire N--1 study, only \pOverallCutPct\% of all
        wind offered is ever curtailed.
\end{itemize}

\end{frame}
